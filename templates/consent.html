Transparent Notice & Ethical Principles
  • Purpose: We collect voluntary daily habit records (sleep duration, activity, self-rated mood) to help you visualize personal longitudinal trends and access support early.
  • No Clinical Diagnosis: This application does not diagnose psychiatric conditions, assign medical labels, or replace mental health clinicians.
  • Human Supervision: Risk assessment scores are treated strictly as supportive signals to guide human counsellor check-ins, never automated decisions.
  • Data Privacy: Administrators only view anonymized, aggregated statistics (with small-group suppression). They cannot view your individual check-ins.
  • Right to Withdraw: You can modify or withdraw consent at any time, or permanently erase your recorded history.
